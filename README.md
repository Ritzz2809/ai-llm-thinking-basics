# ai-llm-thinking-basics
Understanding how Large Language Models think, predict, and fail
The goal is not to build a product, but to deeply understand:
- how AI learns patterns
- why AI predictions are not facts
- how ambiguity and safety matter in AI systems

---

##  Key Concepts Covered
- Difference between AI, ML, DL, and LLMs
- How LLMs predict the next word
- Why AI can hallucinate
- Why safe and careful responses matter

---

##  Project Structure
##  Why This Project Matters
Most AI failures happen not because of bad models, but because:
- questions are unclear
- risks are misunderstood
- models are forced to answer fast instead of safely

This project focuses on understanding these failure points.
