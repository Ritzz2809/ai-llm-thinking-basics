# Observations from AI Experiments

## Experiment 1: Same Question, Different Answers

Observation:
When I asked the same question multiple times, the AI gave slightly different answers.

Learning:
AI does not recall fixed facts like humans.
It predicts the next best word based on probability.

---

## Experiment 2: Small Prompt Change

Observation:
When I changed even one word in the question, the response changed a lot.

Learning:
AI is highly sensitive to prompt wording.
Clear prompts = better output.

---

## Experiment 3: Asking for Confidence

Observation:
AI answered confidently even when the answer might not be fully correct.

Learning:
AI does not "know" truth.
It sounds confident because language patterns are confident.
